# Fresh Adversarial Review and Correction — All-Chats Harmonization Round 2

## Adversarial cases

- unknown `CHAT-*` identifiers;
- duplicated or malformed requirement IDs;
- unassessed legacy Welcome and File 26 contracts;
- stale doctor-ranking recomputation;
- donation influence and hidden manual boost;
- AI impersonation, excessive daily cadence and absent launch-period human review;
- transfer larger than one GiB;
- download without click-time authorization, checksum, recovery or revocation;
- sale of personal data and monetization of security logs;
- missing evidence documentation.

## Closure decision

Cycle 111 encodes each negative path. Every unsafe state remains blocked or unassessed; no silent permissive fallback broadens access, ranking, publication, transfer or download. The repository may be described as a three-plan **code-complete candidate only after exact-head CI passes**. It must not be described as staging-accepted, live-deployed or operational.
