### Primary legal references read (written bibliographic references)

1. **Government of Pakistan, Ministry of Information Technology & Telecommunication**, *Legislations* official classification table, as checked 14 November 2026: entries **Personal Data Protection Bill 2020 – Draft** and **Personal Data Protection Bill May 2023 – Draft** are explicitly marked **Draft**, not Approved. The 2023 text calls itself a proposed future Act: it is not evidence that its requirements are currently enforceable as a comprehensive Pakistani privacy statute. Government source: `www.moitt.gov.pk/Legislations`; official draft `Final Draft Personal Data Protection Bill May 2023`. **Do not turn proposed controller duties, age rules or proposed fines into a legal certification**.
2. **Pakistan, Parliament**, *Prevention of Electronic Crimes Act, 2016* (XL of 2016), as amended; **Prevention of Electronic Crimes (Amendment) Act, 2025**, **Act No. II of 2025**, *The Gazette of Pakistan, Extraordinary*, Part I, Islamabad, **29 January 2025**. The Act received presidential assent and commenced at once according to section 1 of the Gazette publication. Relevant current statutory consequences require clause-specific advice and up-to-date consolidated text. Government source: `na.gov.pk/uploads/documents/679b243193585_457.pdf`; official NCCIA list `nccia.gov.pk/laws.php`. The source's cybercrime prohibitions do **not** by themselves prove every proposed privacy/deletion rule is in force.
3. **Pakistan**, *Unani, Ayurvedic and Homoeopathic Practitioners Act, 1965* (UAH Act), especially practitioner registration provisions **sections 23–24**, and related rules; **National Council for Homoeopathy (NCH)**, official statutory council description and registration responsibilities. Official sources: `nchpakistan.gov.pk` and its Act/Rules repository; parliamentary written answer **National Assembly of Pakistan, 2026**, *Traditional/Complementary Medicine; statutory councils*. The council regulates professional qualifications and registration; a platform's self-declared credential is not proof of verified current NCH registration. Exact profession scope and cross-border practice require qualified advice.
4. **European Parliament and Council**, *Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (General Data Protection Regulation)*, *Official Journal of the European Union*, **27 April 2016**, relevant **Articles 3 (territorial scope), 8 (children's consent where this GDPR basis applies), 9 (special categories, including health data)**. Official legislative text EUR-Lex `eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj`. Mere global access to a Pakistani website does **not automatically establish** Article 3 territorial applicability; assess actual EU targeting, monitoring, establishments, services, and relevant national member-state age law. Article 8 uses a conditional 16-year baseline with member-state lower allowances no less than 13, **not** a universal age rule for all internet sites.
5. **European Data Protection Board**, *Guidelines 3/2018 on the territorial scope of the GDPR (Article 3)*, version 2.1 adopted 12 November 2019 (official EDPB). Use to analyze EU targeting/monitoring signals; do not assert a legal conclusion without collecting actual facts.

## Key safety and legal release boundaries

- Platform identity policy (male 15 / female 12 with guardian rules in earlier product decisions) is a **product proposal**, not a universal legal age threshold. In legal jurisdictions requiring a higher threshold or different parental consent rule, verified local requirements control that operation. The code must not infer guardian consent from signup text alone; enforce by domain and country after counsel verification.
- Health data may be particularly sensitive; do not pass clinical narratives, raw identity evidence or children’s details to open logs, public artifacts, analytic trackers or an AI provider without a legally and technically approved basis.
- Claims of legal compliance, approved practitioner registration, real backup recovery, GDPR compliance and security certification require qualified current evidence. No public Trust claim can be "verified" solely because a repository check is green.
- Drafts are tracked for future readiness; **no draft-only deadline, fee or mandatory consent age** is asserted as current Pakistani law.
- File 24 acts as assurance plane, never replaces native enforcement by File 00/02/07/16/19/20 and original storage owners.
